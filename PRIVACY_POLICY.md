# TeeBox — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: February 2026**

TeeBox ("we", "us", "our", or the "App") is a social community for golfers. This Privacy Policy explains what information TeeBox collects, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. By creating a TeeBox account or using the App, you agree to this Policy.

If you have questions, contact us at **privacy@teebox.app** (or whichever address you set up).

---

## 1. Who we are

TeeBox is operated by the developer whose contact details appear on the Google Play / App Store listing for the App. We are the "data controller" for the personal information described below.

## 2. Information we collect

We only collect information that's needed to run TeeBox. Specifically:

**a. Information you provide when you create an account**
- Email address
- Password (stored only as a bcrypt hash — we never see the plaintext)
- Display name
- Optional: handicap index, home course, bio, avatar photo

**b. Content you create in TeeBox**
- Rounds you log (course name, date, score, par, holes played, fairways, greens in regulation, putts, notes, hole-by-hole scores, optional weather)
- Photos you attach to a round (stored as base64 image data)
- Comments and course reviews you write
- Likes, follows, and wishlist entries you make
- Pinned round selection

**c. Automatically collected technical information**
- IP address (used for rate-limiting login/register/refresh; not tied to your profile)
- Refresh-token session metadata (a random identifier, family identifier, expiry timestamp) used to keep you signed in securely and to detect stolen tokens
- Basic error / crash logs generated by your device's operating system

**d. Information from an external "Share to TeeBox" flow**
- When you tap Share on a round inside another golf app (Garmin Golf, The Grint, etc.), that app forwards the round details (course, score, par, notes) into TeeBox via a deep link. TeeBox only receives the fields the source app sends, and only when you initiate the Share.

We do **not** collect: your precise location, contacts, calendar, microphone audio, camera continuously, health data, advertising identifiers, or biometric data.

## 3. How we use your information

We use your information solely to operate TeeBox:

- Create and secure your account, sign you in, and keep your session alive.
- Show your rounds, reviews, comments, and profile to the people you choose to share them with (i.e., other TeeBox users).
- Compute per-user statistics (rounds played, average score, courses played, mutual friends, achievements).
- Enable search / discovery of golfers and courses.
- Rate-limit abusive traffic and detect refresh-token theft.
- Fix bugs, monitor errors, and improve the App.

We do **not** use your information to serve ads, build advertising profiles, or sell it to third parties.

## 4. What is visible to other TeeBox users

Anything you post in TeeBox is a social post, so treat it as public within the app. Specifically, other signed-in TeeBox users can see:

- Your display name, handicap (if set), home course, bio, and avatar
- Your logged rounds, course reviews, comments, likes, follows, wishlist, and pinned round
- Your friends list and follower / following counts
- Your achievements

We deliberately **do not** show your email address to other users. It's only used for sign-in.

## 5. Photos

When you attach a photo to a round or set an avatar, TeeBox uploads that photo to our server as base64 image data and stores it alongside your account. Individual photos are capped at ~1 MB (avatar ~600 KB) to protect performance. You can remove photos by deleting the round or clearing your avatar in the profile editor at any time.

## 6. Third-party services

TeeBox uses these third-party services strictly to run the app:

- **MongoDB** — stores your account, rounds, comments, reviews, wishlists, and sessions.
- **OpenStreetMap Overpass API** — a public, free data service. If we import a course catalog from OpenStreetMap, we send only a geographic bounding box, never any of your personal data.
- **Google Maps** — when you tap "Open in Maps" on a course, your device opens Google Maps with the course name as a search query. This action happens on your device; Google's own privacy policy then applies to that navigation session.
- **Google Play Services / Apple App Store** — used to distribute the app and handle in-app updates. Their privacy policies apply to that distribution channel.

We do not currently use any advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or crash-reporting SDK. If we add one, we will update this Policy and, where legally required, ask for your consent.

## 7. Data retention

- **Account data** is kept for as long as your account exists.
- **Rounds, comments, reviews, wishlist, follows, likes** are kept until you delete them or delete your account.
- **Refresh-token sessions** are automatically expired after 30 days, and revoked immediately when you sign out.
- **Rate-limit counters** are kept transiently (minutes / hours) and expire automatically.

If you delete your account (see Section 9), we remove your account and content within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

## 8. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including:

- Passwords stored only as **bcrypt** hashes (never plaintext).
- Signed **JWT** access tokens (15-minute lifetime) plus rotating **refresh tokens** with reuse-detection — if a stolen refresh token is used twice, the entire token family is revoked and the attacker is signed out.
- **TLS/HTTPS** on all traffic between your device and our server.
- **Rate limiting** on authentication endpoints to slow brute-force attacks.
- Server-side input validation, image-size caps, and query sanitization to defend against injection and denial-of-service attacks.

No system is 100% secure, but we treat account data with the seriousness it deserves.

## 9. Your rights and choices

You have the right to:

- **Access** your data — everything in-app is visible to you; email us for a machine-readable export.
- **Correct** your data — use the Profile Editor to update display name, handicap, home course, bio, and avatar.
- **Delete** individual rounds, comments, reviews, wishlist entries, or your entire account. Email **privacy@teebox.app** to request account deletion; we will complete the deletion within 30 days.
- **Object** to specific processing or **withdraw consent** where applicable (e.g., you can revoke the "Share to TeeBox" intent by removing TeeBox from your Share sheet in the source app).
- **Portability** — request a JSON export of your rounds, reviews, and comments by emailing us.

Depending on your location (EEA, UK, California, Brazil, etc.), you may have additional rights under GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, LGPD, or similar laws. You can exercise them by contacting **privacy@teebox.app**. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

## 10. Children

TeeBox is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us and we will delete it.

## 11. International transfers

TeeBox's servers may be located outside your country of residence. Where we transfer personal data internationally, we rely on lawful transfer mechanisms (such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses) where required.

## 12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will post the new version in the App and update the "Last updated" date at the top. Continued use of TeeBox after an update means you accept the revised Policy.
